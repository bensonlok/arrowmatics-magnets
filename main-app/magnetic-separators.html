What are magnetic separators?

Magnetic separators are industrial devices that remove ferrous metal (iron, steel, tramp metal) from powders, granules, liquids or conveyed bulk using permanent magnet fields. Plants choose the housing style — grate, bullet, drawer, pulley or plate — to match flow path, cleanability and contaminant size.

Conveyor tramp-iron control

Suspension magnets: overband and suspended plate

Suspension magnets hang above a conveyor belt and pull tramp iron out of the material passing underneath, protecting crushers, screens and downstream equipment. We quote them to your belt and duty.

What is a suspension (overband) magnet?

A suspension magnet is hung on chains or brackets over a conveyor so ferrous pieces are drawn off the burden and stick to the magnet. An overband (self-cleaning) type has a running belt around the magnet that carries the captured iron out to a discharge point. A suspended plate magnet is a fixed plate that is cleaned by hand or by swinging it clear. Overband units suit continuous, heavier tramp-iron loads; plate types suit lighter loads and easier access.

Magnetic bars & grate magnets

Magnetic bars: why full weld?

Full-weld construction is available, built to order for magnetic bars, tubes, threaded square bars and grate magnets on food, pharma and hygiene-sensitive lines. Not every bar model is full weld, so please confirm the build spec with us when we quote.

  • ✓
    No screws, nuts or threads — without full weld, grate and bar magnets can be assembled with screws or nuts to hold parts together. Those fasteners create crevices and threads where product deposit builds up and can cause corrosion, which is a hygiene problem. Full weld removes them, so there is nowhere for deposit to sit.
  • ✓
    No crevices — product, grease and moisture have nowhere to trap, so the bar is easier to clean and inspect.
  • ✓
    Sealed NdFeB core — the magnet is closed off from water, so no rust and no magnet dust getting into your product.
  • ✓
    Wet washdown and high-pressure cleaning — a sealed weld is built to withstand it.
  • ✓
    Vibration and impact resistance — end caps and welds are not likely to loosen the way pressed or screwed-in parts can.
  • ✓
    Smooth, polished weld — no exposed fasteners and no grooves on the product side.

Materials: stainless steel grades 304, 316 and 316L are available for construction. 316 and 316L generally suit more corrosive or washdown-heavy environments. Please confirm the grade at quotation.

Grate magnets ~10,000–13,000 G

Magnetic tubes in a grate frame for hoppers, chutes and free-fall powders. Ideal for food, plastics and fine dry materials. Easy wipe-clean of captured fines. Food-grade stainless options available (304, 316, 316L). Full-weld bar and grate builds available to order for hygiene-sensitive lines.

Bullet magnets Pipeline / pneumatic

A tapered magnet core in a pipe housing for pneumatic or gravity product lines. Captures tramp metal without large pressure drop when sized correctly. Common in plastics compounding and powder transfer.

Drawer magnets Multi-row / hopper

Pull-out drawer with one or more rows of magnetic tubes under hoppers or before extruders. Good for higher throughput and staged capture. Food-grade and industrial builds available.

Magnetic pulleys Conveyor head

Replace or supplement the head pulley on a belt conveyor so ferrous is discharged below while non-magnetic product continues. Used in recycling, aggregates and scrap sorting.

Suspension magnets Overband · suspended plate
