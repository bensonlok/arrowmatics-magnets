## What are magnetic separators?

Magnetic separators remove ferrous metal (iron, steel, tramp metal) from powders, granules, liquids or conveyed bulk using permanent magnet fields. Plants choose housing style — grate, bullet, drawer, pulley or plate — to match flow path, cleanability and contaminant size.

## What is a suspension (overband) magnet?

A suspension magnet is hung on chains or brackets over a conveyor so ferrous pieces are drawn off the burden and stick to the magnet. An overband (self-cleaning) type has a running belt around the magnet that carries the captured iron out; a suspended plate magnet is a fixed plate cleaned by hand. To quote, send belt width, belt speed, burden depth, hanging height and the iron you need to remove.
