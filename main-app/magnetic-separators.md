## What are magnetic separators?

          
Magnetic separators are industrial devices that remove ferrous metal (iron, steel, tramp metal) from powders, granules, liquids or conveyed bulk using permanent magnet fields. Plants choose the housing style — grate, bullet, drawer, pulley or plate — to match flow path, cleanability and contaminant size.

## Separator types we supply

          
Pick by process location. Unsure? Send a photo or line sketch on WhatsApp.

        

        
          
### Grate magnets ~10,000–13,000 G

          
Magnetic tubes in a grate frame for hoppers, chutes and free-fall powders. Ideal for food, plastics and fine dry materials. Easy wipe-clean of captured fines. Food-grade stainless options available.

        
        
          
### Bullet magnets Pipeline / pneumatic

          
A tapered magnet core in a pipe housing for pneumatic or gravity product lines. Captures tramp metal without large pressure drop when sized correctly. Common in plastics compounding and powder transfer.

        
        
          
### Drawer magnets Multi-row / hopper

          
Pull-out drawer with one or more rows of magnetic tubes under hoppers or before extruders. Good for higher throughput and staged capture. Food-grade and industrial builds available.

        
        
          
### Magnetic pulleys Conveyor head

          
Replace or supplement the head pulley on a belt conveyor so ferrous is discharged below while non-magnetic product continues. Used in recycling, aggregates and scrap sorting.

        
        
          
### Plate magnets Chute / suspended

          
Flat magnet plates for chute walls or suspended above belts. Simple, rugged ferrous capture for coarse products and bulk materials.
