Which magnetic separator do I need?

Choose by how the product moves. Then check the build, the cleaning access and the working gap.

Start with how the product moves

Your materialUsual fitWhere it sits
Dry powder or granules falling through a hopper or chuteGrate or drawer magnetAcross the opening or outlet
Material riding on a conveyor beltOverband (self-cleaning) or suspended plate; magnetic pulley at the head endOver or at the end of the belt
Liquid, paste or slurry in a pipeLiquid-line magnetic trapIn-line on the pipe
Powder or pellets in a pipe or pneumatic lineBullet magnetInside the pipe
Chute wall, tank, or a spot where hygiene mattersPlate magnet (full-weld stainless available)Chute wall or above a conveyor
Small parts, OEM use, or heat and corrosionNdFeB or SmCo magnetsMade to your drawing

Then check four things

  • Build: stainless 304, 316 or 316L; full weld is available, built to order, when you want no screws or crevices.
  • Cleaning: hand-clean, pull-out drawer or self-cleaning belt. Leave room to reach it.
  • Working gap: field strength depends on the gap to the product. Typical rare-earth separators we quote sit around 10,000–13,000 gauss at the pole; confirm per application.
  • Temperature: NdFeB suits normal duty; SmCo suits heat and corrosion. Give us your maximum temperature.

Arrowmatics is not itself certified to ISO 22000, HACCP or GMP. This is general guidance; size, grade and build are confirmed at quotation.
