What to send for a magnet quote

A photo plus six facts is usually enough for a first written quote.

Send these

  • Photo of the spot. One wide shot and one close shot of the hopper, chute, pipe or conveyor. A sketch is fine too.
  • Material: powder, granules, pellets, liquid or slurry, and what it is.
  • Size: opening, belt width, pipe size, hanging height or burden depth, whichever applies.
  • Flow: rate, particle size and moisture.
  • The problem: what iron you are finding (rust, wear fragments, tramp iron) and where it does harm.
  • Temperature and any corrosive or washdown conditions.
  • Cleaning and hygiene: how you clean now, and whether you need a full-weld stainless finish.
  • Quantity and any drawing you already have.

Photo tips

  • Put a tape measure or ruler in the shot so we can see the scale.
  • Circle or mark the problem area on the photo or sketch.
  • Photos are resized in your browser before upload; you can also send them on WhatsApp.

What you get back

Plain-language advice on which type fits, then a written quote with size, grade and build confirmed. We publish no RM list prices. We aim to reply within 1–3 hours in Malaysia business hours; that is a target, not a guarantee.

Arrowmatics is not itself certified to ISO 22000, HACCP or GMP. This is general guidance; size, grade and build are confirmed at quotation.
