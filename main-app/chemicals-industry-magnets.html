Quick answers

Which magnet for a chemical powder or liquid line?

Bullet magnets suit pipes and pneumatic lines, grate or drawer magnets suit hoppers and chutes, and liquid-line traps suit liquids and slurries. Send the material, size, temperature and a photo for a recommendation.

Which stainless grade for corrosive duty?

304, 316 and 316L are available. 316 / 316L generally suit more corrosive or washdown-heavy conditions. This is general guidance with no test data; confirm the grade for your chemicals at quotation.
