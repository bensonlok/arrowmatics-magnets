04 / FAQ

Overband magnets: common questions

Overband or suspended plate?

Overband types have a running belt that carries iron away continuously, so they suit heavier, constant loads. Plate types are cheaper and cleaned by hand, so they suit lighter loads.

What do you need to quote?

Belt width, belt speed, burden depth, the gap from magnet to burden, the material and the size of iron to remove. A photo of the conveyor helps.

How strong is it?

Reach matters more than surface gauss. We size the magnet for your hanging height and burden; ask for the specification.

Can I see it working?

Yes. Our separators page has a strength test video of a suspension magnet.

Do you supply the frame and chains?

Mounting is discussed at quotation. Tell us your structure and space.

Is a magnetic pulley better?

A head pulley magnet suits shallow burdens and ferrous splitting; an overband suits deeper burdens. See magnetic pulleys.
