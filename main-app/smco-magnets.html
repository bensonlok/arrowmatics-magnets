What are SmCo magnets?

SmCo (samarium cobalt) magnets are rare-earth permanent magnets made mainly from samarium and cobalt. People search for them when NdFeB is not stable enough in heat or corrosive duty. Arrowmatics Magnets (Arrowmatics AI Sdn Bhd) supplies SmCo for Malaysian OEMs, MRO and industrial assemblies — written quote via WhatsApp, no online cart.

SmCo vs NdFeB — which one do I need?

NdFeB is usually stronger at room temperature and cheaper for the same pull. SmCo keeps useful magnetism at higher temperatures and resists corrosion better (often with lighter coating). If your magnet sits near motors, ovens, oil, chemicals, or outdoor heat cycles, ask for SmCo. Need max strength on a cool fixture? Start with NdFeB N35–N52.

Why buyers choose SmCo

High temperature

SmCo grades are chosen when NdFeB would demagnetise in heat. Confirm max working temperature on your RFQ.

Corrosion resistance

Better inherent corrosion behaviour than bare NdFeB — useful in humid or chemical plant environments.

Stable over time

Low long-term flux loss for sensors, aerospace-style fixtures and precision industrial assemblies.

Sensors & motors

Hall sensors, coupling magnets, aerospace/defence-adjacent OEM parts, and hot-spot motor designs.

Oil & process plant

When coatings alone are not enough for NdFeB, SmCo is the usual rare-earth upgrade path.
