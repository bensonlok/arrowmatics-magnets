## What are NdFeB magnets?

**NdFeB** (neodymium-iron-boron) magnets are the strongest common sintered rare-earth permanent magnets used in industry. Arrowmatics Magnets supplies grades **N35 through N52**.

## Is N52 the strongest commercial grade?

Among widely stocked sintered NdFeB grades, **N52** sits at the high end of the energy-product range (~52 MGOe). Higher “N” numbers are stronger at room temperature but may have lower max operating temperature than specialty high-temp grades (H, SH, UH). We match grade to duty cycle, temperature and coating.
