# Lifting magnets for steel handling

      
Permanent and electro lifting magnets for plate, billet and scrap. Capacities sized to your load with a safety factor of about 3:1.

      

    
  

  
    
      
        
          
## What are lifting magnets?

          
Lifting magnets are industrial devices that pick up ferromagnetic loads — steel plate, billets, beams or scrap — using a magnetic field instead of slings alone. They come as permanent (no power to hold) or electro (power on to magnetise) designs. Arrowmatics Magnets supplies both for Malaysian fabrication yards, warehouses and mills.

        
        
          
## What safety factor do you use for lifting magnets?

          
We size lifting magnets around a safety factor of about 3:1 relative to rated load under stated conditions (air gap, material thickness, surface condition). Always follow your site safety procedures, crane ratings and local regulations. Tell us load weight, plate thickness and lift style for a written recommendation.

        
      
    

    
      
        
          
## Permanent vs electro

        
        
          
            
### Permanent lifting magnets

            
Mechanical on/off (often lever or cam). No power needed to maintain hold — useful where power fails mid-lift must not drop the load. Suited to plate handling in workshops and yards when air gaps are controlled.

            
              No hold power
              Plate / billet
              Workshop / yard
            
          
          
            
### Electro lifting magnets

            
Energised coils create the field. Good for scrap, irregular loads and duty where remote on/off is needed. Require reliable power and typically a backup or control scheme per site rules.

            
              Powered field
              Scrap / irregular
              Remote control
