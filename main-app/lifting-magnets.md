## What are lifting magnets?

Industrial devices that pick up ferromagnetic loads using a magnetic field. **Permanent** (no power to hold) or **electro** (power on to magnetise). Supplied for Malaysian fabrication yards, warehouses and mills.

## Safety factor

We size lifting magnets around a **safety factor of about 3:1** relative to rated load under stated conditions (air gap, material thickness, surface condition). Follow site safety procedures, crane ratings and local regulations.

## Permanent vs electro

- **Permanent** — mechanical on/off; no hold power; plate / billet in workshops and yards
- **Electro** — powered field; scrap / irregular loads; remote on/off where power and controls allow
